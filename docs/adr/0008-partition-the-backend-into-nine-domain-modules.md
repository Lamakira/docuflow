# Partition the backend into nine domain modules

DocuFlow's backend is divided into nine modules — Identity & Access, Workspace, Clients & Sales, Projects, Time, Activity, Knowledge, Billing, and Notifications — with search/retrieval behind its own seam owning index artifacts. Policy lives with its enforcement engine (Tracking Policy in Activity, Work Schedule in Time), transcripts are Knowledge content while embeddings are index artifacts, and a Billable Seat is the active Membership itself rather than a ledger row. Entitlement checks are synchronous cross-module queries while consequences ride transactional-outbox events; workspace provisioning is the single sanctioned synchronous cross-module command chain, with compensation; read models start as composed queries and are materialized per-module only on measured need. A central-settings module and a seat ledger were rejected because they would split rules from their interpreters and duplicate membership truth.
