# Own audit, privacy, and compliance controls behind platform ports

DocuFlow records security-relevant actions — authentication, membership and role lifecycle, Tracking Policy and Workspace AI Policy changes, billing and entitlement changes, sensitive exports and downloads, deletions and restores, credential and Service Account lifecycle, device enrollment, privileged operator access, and AI gateway calls — as PII-minimized, ID-referencing Audit Events in one append-only PostgreSQL table behind a thin audit port, carrying workspace and platform scopes in one log with mutation grants revoked and closed segments exported to immutable object storage as the evidence copy. DocuFlow operates as GDPR processor for Workspace content and controller for account, billing, and platform telemetry: account deletion runs a guided flow — every owned workspace transferred or deleted first, then a cancelable grace window — erases controller-side data, pseudonymizes Memberships in place so operational and audit records keep referential integrity, and notifies each affected workspace, while workspace-content erasure remains the customer controller's duty served by existing Trash/purge tools plus a self-serve account-level export. Six fixed platform Data Classes — Public, Workspace-Operational, Sensitive-Evidence, Identity, Financial, Secret — drive the control matrix; retention rules live with their owning modules and execute as scheduled idempotent jobs; backups keep a bounded window and an ID-only Erasure Journal is replayed after every restore so resurrected data is re-purged. Encryption is provider-managed at rest plus TLS in transit, with KMS-backed envelope encryption reserved for Secret-class fields and DocuFlow-issued credentials stored as one-time-shown hashes; per-workspace BYOK is a named enterprise reassessment trigger, not v1 architecture. Platform secrets come from the chosen host's managed secret store, injected per environment with versioned signing keys — retiring the legacy ephemeral JWT secret and the MCP_API_KEY admin-impersonation path. Operators act through a separate audited surface inside the monolith using platform-staff principals from a dedicated identity instance, with no standing access to workspace content, time-boxed workspace-visible Support Access Grants, and loud audited break-glass; incident response pairs a documented 72-hour breach-notification runbook with Legal Hold as a purge-blocking port primitive; vendors enter through a published Subprocessor Registry gated on signed DPAs, recorded regions, and no-training terms; the compliance control matrix lives in-repo mapping each SOC 2 / ISO 27001 control to its implementing decision and evidence source, with quarterly access reviews run from Identity & Access queries. Hash-chained audit logs, auditing every domain write, a separate admin application, workspace-configurable classification, a central retention engine, field-level encryption of general content, auto-purging another controller's records on account deletion, and adopting a compliance-automation platform now were all rejected.
