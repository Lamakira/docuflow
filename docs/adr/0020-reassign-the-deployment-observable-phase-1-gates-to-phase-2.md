# Reassign the deployment-observable Phase 1 gates to Phase 2

Amends ADR-0017's phase-gate assignment. Four of the nine Phase 1 gates recorded in `docs/migration/phase-1-stabilization.md` — G3 (CI/CD with synthetic staging), G4 (migrations applied as a gated pre-deploy step), G8 (OpenTelemetry baseline), and G9 (persistent versioned desktop signing key) — cannot reach **Verified** from repository or CI evidence, because each one's remaining proof is a property of a running deployment: a service deployed, a migration executed against a deployed database, telemetry received by a collector, a secret surviving a restart. ADR-0018 moved all deployment into an isolated parallel environment, and ADR-0017 makes standing that environment up the work of Phase 2. Phase 1 therefore contains gates whose proof Phase 1 is structurally unable to produce. The four gates move to Phase 2 and are carried as explicit acceptance criteria on issues [#54](https://github.com/Lamakira/docuflow/issues/54) (G3, G4), [#55](https://github.com/Lamakira/docuflow/issues/55) (G8), and [#56](https://github.com/Lamakira/docuflow/issues/56) (G9); the Phase 2 evidence record ([#61](https://github.com/Lamakira/docuflow/issues/61)) reports their final status. Phase 1 closes on its repository-observable gates — G1, G2, G5, G6, G7 Verified — with the four reassigned gates named and unproven, not silently absorbed.

The reassignment is an accounting change and grants nothing. The four gates keep their ADR-0017 wording and their evidentiary standard, no status improves by moving, and the Phase 1 record continues to state each one's missing operational proof; a reader auditing G8 finds the same unproved exporter, now with the issue that owns it. Two constraints hold the change honest: no Phase 1 gate whose proof is repository-observable may be reassigned, and Phase 2 cannot close while any reassigned gate is still unproved — the Phase 1 debt and the Phase 2 milestone rise or fall together. Separately, the production-oriented `.github/workflows/desktop-release.yml` conflict that the Phase 1 record flagged is not a gate and is not reassigned here; it carries its own disposition ticket ([#60](https://github.com/Lamakira/docuflow/issues/60)). Holding Phase 1 open across the whole of Phase 2 was rejected as leaving a milestone open for a phase without changing what is true or who is accountable; marking the four gates Verified on the strength of merged implementation and a green CI run was rejected outright, since it is exactly the inference the Phase 1 record forbids; and folding them into Phase 3 or later was rejected because Phase 2 is the first phase in which a deployment exists to observe.
